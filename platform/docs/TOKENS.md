# Tokens: what they are for

The owner's direction (October 2026): keep as much of Yavaya free as it is
now. Tokens are never needed to take part. They are an optional boost for
members who want more reach, especially a seller whose listing is getting no
attention. Prices live in `billable_actions` and settings in `system_settings`,
so all of this can be retuned without a deploy (see CONFIGURATION.md).

## Free, always

Signing up, browsing, searching, messaging, publishing within the free
allowance (5 open Mercadito listings, 2 open Trabajo offers), applying for
work, ordering on YavayaGo (no commission), asking for and answering service
requests, Comunidad, Santuario, Animales, Espacio Violeta, Roxi, profile
customization, saved searches, and the first change of display name.

## What tokens buy

| Use | Price | Who it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Renew a Mercadito listing: back to the top of the newest, once a day | 1 | A seller whose listing has slipped down |
| Feature a listing for 7 days, labelled "Destacado" | 3 | A seller who wants to be seen first |
| Feature a Trabajo offer for 7 days | 3 | An employer hiring fast |
| Feature a YavayaGo store for 7 days | 5 | A business wanting more orders |
| Each open Mercadito listing beyond 5 | 1 | Resellers and dealers: people earning from volume |
| Each open Trabajo offer beyond 2 | 3 | Employers with many openings |
| Change display name again (after the free one) | 1 | Anyone who wants a new name |

Renewing is the cheap first step; featuring is the strong one. The cooldown on
renewal keeps the two distinct: staying on top for good is featuring, and
featured listings are always labelled, so paid placement is never passed off
as relevance.

## Nobody has to buy

Every member gets tokens without paying: 2 a day for the first 7 days after
verifying their email (14), and 3 for each friend who joins and verifies (the
friend gets 2), up to the monthly cap. That is enough to renew or feature a
few times before ever spending money. When a member cannot pay for a boost,
the page offers inviting friends first and buying second.

## Where tokens are offered

They are offered at the moment they are useful, never as a gate:

- On a listing's own page, the seller sees "Renovar" and "Destacar" side by
  side, with the price, their balance and, for renewal, when it is next
  allowed.
- "Mis publicaciones" lists open listings that have gone 7 days without a
  renewal and links each one to those two options. It goes by age only:
  Yavaya does not count views, so it never claims a listing had no visits.
- Settings says before the button whether a name change is free or costs a
  token.
- The public Tokens page lists every use and price from the live values.

## Ideas not built

Kept here so they are decided, not drifted into: renewing Trabajo offers and
YavayaGo stores, a token bundle that renews automatically, and highlighting a
listing in its category. Each would follow the same rules: optional, labelled,
priced in `billable_actions`, and never in place of something free today.
