Reputation

Your profile is worth what you have done

Every action builds reputation, and reputation works in every district. In the long run it is worth more than any token.

Trust score

From 0 to 1000

Everyone has a trust score that summarises their history on Yavaya.

The algorithm rewards consistency, not popularity. The exact formulas are configurable and will be published once they are defined.

Adds

  • Account age
  • Successful sales and purchases
  • Completed projects and deliveries
  • Positive reviews
  • Verification level
  • Community contributions

Subtracts

  • Fraud reports
  • Disputes
  • Refund history
  • Suspicious behaviour flags
