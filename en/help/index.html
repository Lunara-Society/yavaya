Help

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, it probably has no answer yet: Yavaya is under construction.

What is Yavaya?

A digital ecosystem for Central America: buying and selling, ordering delivery, finding work, supporting causes, adopting animals and being there for each other in community — all with one account and one reputation.

Can I create an account yet?

Not yet. The platform foundation is built, but public registration opens once email delivery is configured, because without it we cannot verify your account. The Status page shows this as it stands.

When does Yavaya open?

We do not give a date we cannot keep. The roadmap shows the order in which districts will open.

What does it cost?

Signing up, creating an account, viewing listings, reading content and receiving donations are always free.

What is paid is optional: Work subscriptions for professionals and visibility options. Nothing can be paid for today.

Are tokens cryptocurrency?

No. They are credits for taking part in the platform: posting, featuring, being seen. They are not an investment, cannot be traded, and there is no token market.

Do I have to use tokens?

Never. Cash is always the default and tokens are always optional.

How do you prevent scams?

With levelled verification, one account per person, visible reputation, review of listings and causes, limits for new accounts and visible moderation. The Trust page explains it.

Does Yavaya take a commission on donations?

No. Impact has 0% Yavaya commission.

Which countries will it cover?

Guatemala, Belize, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, Costa Rica and Panama.

What does "DEMO" mean?

An illustrative example. It is not a real person or listing, it never counts in any statistic, and it disappears once real activity arrives.
