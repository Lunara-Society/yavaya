Adoption

A complete process, not a click

  1. ApplicationTell us who you are and which animal you are interested in.
  2. Identity verificationTo protect the animal and whoever is giving it up.
  3. Household informationSpace, family and routine.
  4. Suitability reviewIs this the right home for this animal?
  5. InterviewWhere appropriate.
  6. Approval and agreementClear commitments in writing.
  7. Follow-upAfter the adoption, to make sure everything is going well.
Adoption is not instant, on purpose. Every step is recorded. Supporting animals on Yavaya is free.
