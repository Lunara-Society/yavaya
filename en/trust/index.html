The trust layer

Trust is the product

Most marketplaces compete on features, design and price. Yavaya competes on trust. If Yavaya is the safest platform in Central America, everything else gets easier.

Before asking can this be built?, we ask: can this be abused? Protection first. Then features.

Fraud defence

What we watch for

Duplicate accounts

One person, one account. We combine several signals with human review; we never block on a single signal.

Fake listings and causes

Stolen images, impossible prices, stories with nothing behind them.

Token abuse

Every source of free tokens has limits. They can never be farmed without end.

New accounts

Every new account goes through a period of closer monitoring.

Suspicious behaviour

Unusual usage patterns open a review, not an automatic penalty.

Community reports

Reporting a scam that is confirmed is recognised with reputation.

We do not publish exact detection thresholds: explaining them would help anyone trying to evade them.

Guardians · future feature

Citizens who look after others

  • Review flagged reports.
  • Spot scams before moderation does.
  • Help people who are new.
  • Support community moderation.

They are recognised with reputation, status and a Guardian badge — not large token rewards. The motivation is trust, not money.
