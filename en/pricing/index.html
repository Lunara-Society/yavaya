Pricing

Sustainable without being predatory

The mission is not to extract the most money from each person. It is to create the largest trusted economic ecosystem in Central America. Revenue is the result, not the goal.

Always free

What Yavaya will never charge for

  • Signing up
  • Creating an account
  • Receiving donations
  • Viewing listings
  • Reading content
  • Basic verification
  • Community support, causes and animal welfare

The founder rule

Every pricing decision passes three tests

  1. Does it increase trust?If it damages trust, it is not built.
  2. Does it improve the experience?For people who pay and people who do not.
  3. Would we do it for half the money?If the answer is no, it is not built.

Friction kills trust. And trust is everything.
