Demo content

How we handle examples

  1. Phase 1Demo content is shown, clearly marked DEMO.
  2. Phase 2Real people arrive and their content takes priority.
  3. Phase 3With enough activity, all demo content is removed.
  • DEMO content never counts in any statistic.
  • It never triggers notifications.
  • It always expires.
  • Live activity shows only real events. If there is nothing real, it shows nothing.
The metric that matters

Successful trust transactions

Not revenue. Not downloads. Not registered accounts.

It is every sale, purchase, delivery, donation, job, adoption or act of community support that goes well. If that number grows, Yavaya is on the right track.
