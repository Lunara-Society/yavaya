The objective is to transform the existing Yavaya ecosystem into an exceptionally advanced, mobile-first, visually immersive and intelligently adaptive application while preserving its existing functionality, business rules, users, database, security and commercial architecture.

ROXI'S FUNDAMENTAL INTELLIGENCE

Roxi must not depend on predefined animations for individual products or listings.

She must understand the structure, meaning, available media, category, attributes and permitted actions associated with dynamically changing Yavaya content.

When a completely new listing is published, Roxi must automatically be capable of interacting with it through her general intelligence, semantic understanding, spatial awareness and reusable behavioral primitives.

Her behavior must be composed dynamically according to the user's intent, current screen, available data, content geometry and interaction permissions.

She must understand unfamiliar content and adapt gracefully when information or visual assets are incomplete.

She must never invent product specifications, seller credentials, prices, availability, geographical information, ratings or transaction results.
